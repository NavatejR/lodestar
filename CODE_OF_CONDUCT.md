# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

* being kind, and assuming the other person is trying to be helpful;
* accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and giving it the same way;
* focusing on what is best for the project and its users, not on winning;
* taking responsibility for what you say, apologising where appropriate, and
  learning from mistakes;
* using inclusive language in issues, reviews, commits and documentation.

Behaviour that does not:

* sexualised language or attention, or unwelcome advances of any kind;
* trolling, insults, derogatory or discriminatory comments, or personal and
  political attacks;
* public or private harassment of any kind;
* publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
* anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, and any chat or forum the project runs — and whenever someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at the address in `Cargo.toml`.
Reports are reviewed promptly and treated confidentially; the reporter's
identity is not revealed without their consent. The maintainers will take any
action they deem appropriate, from a private warning to a permanent ban from
the project, and will explain the reasoning to the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may be
removed from the project by the other maintainers.
